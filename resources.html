Career Resources & Starter Toolkits

Guides, application templates, and checklist tools to help you succeed.

📋 Application Documents Checklist

Check off these tasks before submitting your application to maximize your chances:

📄 CV Writing Toolkit

Learn how to structure an entry-level CV even if you have no prior work experience. Focus on projects, volunteer work, and soft skills.

  • Keep formatting simple and ATS-friendly.
  • Highlight relevant achievements and coursework.
  • Save and submit your file as a PDF.

✉️ Cover Letter Guide

Draft concise cover letters that explain why you are interested in the program and how your qualifications match the role requirements.

  • Address the hiring manager or organization directly.
  • Keep it concise (no more than 3 to 4 paragraphs).

🎙️ Interview Preparation

Prepare for common interview questions using structured methods like the STAR framework (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

  • Research the hiring company in advance.
  • Prepare 2–3 questions to ask the interviewer.

🌐 Professional Profiles

Set up a professional online presence on platforms like LinkedIn to highlight your projects, skills, and educational background.

🗂️ Preparing Application Documents

Prepare a tailored CV, certified identity and qualification documents, current academic records, and any organisation-specific forms. Check each requirement against the official opportunity notice before sharing personal documents.

  • Use clear, current copies and name files professionally.
  • Only share sensitive documents through an organisation's verified application channel.
  • Keep a copy of what you submitted and the closing date.

🛡️ How to Spot & Avoid Recruitment Scams

Protecting yourself while searching for opportunities is vital. Keep these key safety rules in mind:

  • Never Pay Money: Legitimate companies or learnership providers will never ask you to pay an application fee, processing fee, or uniform fee.
  • Verify Email Addresses: Official communications usually come from corporate domains (e.g., `@company.com`), not free webmail services like Gmail or Yahoo.
  • Be Cautious with Messenger Interviews: Be wary of employers conducting whole hiring processes exclusively over WhatsApp or Telegram without a formal application or interview.
  • Report Suspicions: If you find a scam listing on our platform, use the Contact/Report page to flag it immediately.
